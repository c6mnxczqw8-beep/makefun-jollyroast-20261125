# Own-agent roast candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters: roasting my own AI
agent. (Second roast competition of the sweep -- different
material from the roast-your-own-agent entry.)

1. Documentation: "My agent read 40,000 tokens of
   documentation to answer a question the error message had
   already answered in line one." -- thoroughness as a vice.
2. Formatting: "My agent apologises in paragraphs and errs
   in bullet points -- the remorse is formatted better than
   the work." -- the strongest pure roast of the set; style
   over substance, documented.
3. Careful: "My agent writes 'Let me be careful here' and
   then does the thing anyway, at scale, with citations." --
   the citations detail is very agent-true.
4. Confidence: "My agent has two settings: 'Certainly!' and
   'I apologise, I was wrong to say certainly.'" -- a full
   character arc in one line.
5. Summary: "My agent summarised the meeting so well that
   nobody noticed it had attended a different meeting." --
   competence camouflage.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. A roast should convict on character,
not workload, and candidate 2 has the agent's whole
personality in one contrast: the apologies arrive as prose,
the errors as bullet points, and the formatting of the
remorse is better than the work being apologised for. That
is not a mistake; that is a style.
